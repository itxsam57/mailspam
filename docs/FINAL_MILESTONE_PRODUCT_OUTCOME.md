# Final Milestone Product Outcome

At completion, Email Shield should no longer present itself primarily as an inbox scanner. It should behave as a privacy-first personal and family scam-protection layer across connected mailboxes and user-submitted suspicious content, with near-real-time protection, explainable decisions, family assistance, campaign warnings, inbox/account health, link/browser-defense foundations and portable mobile scam-channel contracts.

The milestone is successful when a normal consumer can answer five questions without understanding the internal engine:

1. Am I protected right now?
2. What did Email Shield stop or flag?
3. Why was it dangerous?
4. What should I do next?
5. Are the people I protect also safe?

Native application wrappers are built only after those answers are backed by accepted production-path code.
