# Final Milestone Competitor Research Source Categories

Research for this milestone is restricted to public product pages, public support documentation, public security/research material and independently observable product behavior.

Competitors reviewed for feature-pattern coverage include Guardio, Bitdefender, McAfee, Norton, Aura, Clean Email, Google/Gmail and Microsoft/Outlook/Defender.

Marketing accuracy claims are not imported as Email Shield claims. Email Shield must establish its own accuracy, privacy, performance and false-positive evidence through its Regression Vault and acceptance gates.
